Built for Mac. Honest about Face ID.

Peek is a hardened fork of Glance — same notch UI, smoother enrollment, and a stronger anti-photo core.

  • Seamless enroll — turn your head; no exact-angle hunting.
  • 3D geometry vault — multi-pose landmark map, encrypted on-device.
  • MiniFASNet — silent anti-spoof to reject photos and screens.
  • On-device only — face data and password never leave your Mac.

Macs have no TrueDepth. Peek blocks printed and phone-screen photos with high confidence, but a high-quality video replay can still fool a webcam. Convenience unlock — not a Touch ID replacement.
